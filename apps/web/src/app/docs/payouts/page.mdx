# Get paid

Each charge adds 95% to your app's earnings. The network keeps 5% to pay for gas. Refunds come out of your earnings.

## Withdraw

Click **Withdraw** on your app's page in the [dashboard](/dashboard), or:

```ts
await credits.payout();
// { usdt: "12.34", to: "you.testnet", explorer }
```

You get USDT on NEAR, at your payout account. The network pays the gas.

## Reports

The dashboard shows your revenue, earnings, paying users and every transaction, with a one-click CSV export. From code:

```ts
await credits.activity(); // every charge, hold, refund and payout
await credits.exportCsv(); // the same, as CSV
```
