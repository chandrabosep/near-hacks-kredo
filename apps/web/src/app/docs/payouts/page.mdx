# Payouts and reporting

## Earnings

Each charge adds its amount, minus the 5% network fee, to your app's **unpaid earnings** in the vault. Refunds come out of them.

## Withdraw

Click **Withdraw** on your app's page, or:

```ts
const p = await credits.payout();
// { usdt: "12.34", to: "0x…", txHash, explorer }
```

Your unpaid earnings are paid in USDT, on Whitechain, to the app's payout address. The network pays the gas.

## Reporting

| Call | Returns |
|---|---|
| `activity()` | Every charge, hold, capture, release, refund and payout, newest first, each with its transaction |
| `exportCsv()` | The same as CSV, for your accountant. Also on the dashboard: **Export CSV** |
| `gas()` | How many transactions your app caused and what they cost in WBT. Paid by the network, never by you |

The dashboard shows revenue, earnings after fees and refunds, unpaid earnings, paying users and gas for each app.
